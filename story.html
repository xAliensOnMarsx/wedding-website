Our Story

From coworkers to forever.

❦

Every great love story begins somewhere, and ours began at Ried's Food Barn.

What started as working together slowly turned into friendship. Before long, Levi asked Addy to grab ranch pizza from Mickey's, and one simple meal became the beginning of something much bigger.

As the years passed, their friendship grew into love, and after a fun evening of bingo at the Ischua Fire Hall, Levi asked Addy the question that would change both of their lives forever.

Now they can't wait to celebrate the next chapter of their story with the people they love most on May 22, 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

❦

We are so excited to celebrate with you. Here are answers to some common questions about our wedding day.

The ceremony will take place on May 22, 2027, at Fresh Fire Worship Center, located at 3036 NY-417, Olean, New York.

Guests may begin arriving at 3:30 PM, and the ceremony will begin at 4:00 PM.

The reception will be held at the Cuba VFW Reception Hall, located at 5425 NY-305, Cuba, New York.

Dinner will begin at 6:30 PM. Guests will have time to travel from the ceremony location to the reception hall before dinner begins.

We recommend semi-formal or dressy casual attire. Please choose something comfortable that you will enjoy wearing throughout the ceremony, dinner, and dancing.

Please refer to your invitation for the names of the invited guests in your party. Due to limited space, we are only able to accommodate guests included on the invitation.

Please check your invitation to see whether children are included in your party. We appreciate your understanding as we work within the venue's capacity.

There will not be a formal cocktail hour. The wedding party will take photographs after the ceremony, and dinner will begin at 6:30 PM.

Parking is available at both the ceremony and reception locations. We recommend arriving early so you have plenty of time to park and find your seat.

We kindly ask that guests keep phones and cameras put away during the ceremony so everyone can be fully present. You are welcome to take photos during the reception.

If you have any additional questions before the wedding, please contact Levi Otero by text message only at (716) 904-1420.

We cannot wait to celebrate with you.
